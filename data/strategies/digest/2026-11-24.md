Qantas has decommissioned the inbox that provided a lifeline to customers impacted by Classic Reward ticketing failures. Photo: Matt Graham.
Qantas has quietly shut down the Reward Ticketing Escalations inbox that Australian Frequent Flyer helped to establish .
Qantas originally launched this service in January 2023 to address complaints from frequent flyers about systemic Classic Flight Reward ticketing failures.
Why the inbox existed in the first place
In the months and years leading up to January 2023, countless Qantas Frequent Flyer members had redeemed their points for flights on partner airlines – only to later find that their flights had been cancelled without their knowledge. This was happening because Qantas was failing to issue those tickets (or reissue when required) before the partner airline’s ticketing deadline.
Many people only discovered there was a problem after turning up at the airport and being unable to check in, through no fault of their own.
At the time, many Qantas Frequent Flyer members who were aware of these ticketing issues were also struggling to get in contact with a competent Qantas agent in a timely manner.

### HCF Discontinuing "HCF Thank You" (Lifestyle Rewards) Gift Card Platform on 30 June 2026?
- source: ozbargain_forum · relevance 3 · <https://www.ozbargain.com.au/node/955429>

After logging in, there is a banner at the top of the HCF Thank You page:
From 1 July 2026, HCF Thank You offers will no longer be available in the My Membership app or online member services. To access your new member offers now, download the HCF Thank You app.
HCF has maintained two "Thank You" gift card platforms for a while now - one by Lifestyle Rewards, and the newer one by eGift It. The former (Blackhawk/Lifestyle Rewards) is the one most would be familiar with - where you get upfront savings on a range of gift cards, accessed via a web browser. The new one is accessed via an app and doesn't seem to provide upfront discounts for gift cards - rather the savings are added to a balance that can only be redeemed once the balance reaches $20 (either withdraw to a bank account or pay HCF premiums).
It's a shame if it's going away because I've really appreciated having access to Lifestyle Rewards.

The KrisFlyer Spontaneous Escapes deals are a monthly staple of Singapore Airlines’ frequent flyer program.
Each month, Singapore Airlines normally offers 30% off the KrisFlyer miles required for last-minute award flights on a range of different routes. You can use these to book Saver awards at Promo rates in Economy, Premium Economy and Business Class, for travel during the following month.
Singapore Airlines has just released its Spontaneous Escapes deals for November 2026. Until 30 November 2026, you can get 30% off the KrisFlyer miles on selected award flights for travel during December 2026.
This month’s promotion includes flights from all Australian cities, including Sydney’s WSI airport from Singapore. Also, it includes flights to various destinations in Asia, USA and Europe, plus fifth freedom flights between Frankfurt to New York and Tokyo to Los Angeles.

Remember when a double status credit offer from Qantas or Virgin Australia was actually something to get excited about?
In October 2026, both airlines launched yet another round of bonus status credit promotions. Velocity offered double status credits or points, while Qantas Business Rewards ran its own double status credit promotion.
These offers are normally fantastic news for frequent flyers looking to earn or retain status. But with promotions now appearing almost every month, I can’t help but wonder whether Qantas and Virgin Australia are killing the very thing that made them so effective.
When double status credits were worth waiting for
In the years immediately following COVID-19, one of the most common questions on the AFF forum I saw was when the next double status credit (DSC) offer would arrive.
It became such a frequent question that members even had a templated response ready to go! Or, some now choose to play bingo with inevitable follow ups…

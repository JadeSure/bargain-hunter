Getting an upgrade to Jetstar Business Class is now easier than ever. Jetstar has launched two new ways to move up front from Economy: bidding for a seat with BidCash, or using Qantas Points to request a Jetstar Classic Upgrade.
With Jetstar refitting its Boeing 787s with more than twice as many Business Class seats, your chances of scoring an upgrade are only getting better.
Here is how each option works, what it costs and which one offers the best value.
Which Jetstar flights can you upgrade?
Jetstar only operates a Business Class cabin on its wide-body Boeing 787 aircraft. These operate flights from Australia to Singapore, Seoul, Colombo, Phuket, Osaka and Ho Chi Minh City. Jetstar’s Airbus A320 and A321neo aircraft don’t have a Business Class cabin.
Jetstar Boeing 787. Photo: Jetstar.
Jetstar is in the process of refitting all 11 of its 787s with a 44-seat Business Class cabin (up from 21 seats), plus Wi-Fi. The first refitted aircraft entered service in April 2026, and more seats up front should mean better odds of an upgrade.

Australian Frequent Flyer
Australian Frequent Flyer - Tips, Tricks and Chat for Australian Frequent Flyers
Qatar Airways “Qsuite” Business Class. Photo: Qatar Airways.
Qatar Airways Privilege Club is one of seven loyalty programs that uses Avios as its points currency. Like British Airways Club , Iberia Plus and Finnair Plus , Qatar Airways’ loyalty program also sells Avios directly to members.
By buying Avios cheaply and redeeming them for high-value rewards, you could save substantial amounts of money on Business Class flights. This includes the legendary Qatar Airways Qsuite .
Since you can transfer Avios between programs on a 1:1 basis , it makes sense to buy Avios from whichever program is offering the best price. That won’t always be Qatar Airways Privilege Club, but it could be when a discount or bonus is running.
How to buy Qatar Avios
You can purchase or gift Qatar Privilege Club Avios on the Qatar Airways website.
The minimum purchase is 1,000 Avios, and you can purchase in various increments:
1,000 – 26,000 Avios in 1,000 Avios increments
26,000 – 100,000 Avios in 2,000 Avios increments
100,000 – 250,000 Avios in 5,000 Avios increments
The maximum purchase limit is 250,000 Avios before any bonuses.
If you register your account in Australia, Qatar Airways lets you buy Avios in AUD. Note that the prices may fluctuate slightly, depending on exchange rate changes.

Airbus’ Project Sunrise test aircraft lands in Melbourne after a non-stop flight from Toulouse, France. Photo: Qantas.
An Airbus A350-1000ULR operated the world’s longest flight by a commercial aircraft in July 2026, flying eastbound from Australia to France without stopping . The record-breaking flight took 24 hours and 24 minutes.
This is one of the planes that Qantas will start using next year to operate non-stop passenger flights from Sydney to London. That flight could take up to 21 hours.
Qantas believes customers will pay a premium for the non-stop service – even in Economy.
Whether enough people are willing to pay extra to avoid a refuelling stop en-route remains to be seen. But there is every reason to believe they will, because of the thousands of people who already fly every day from Australia to Europe, the majority choose the shortest connection times. Most people stop somewhere in Asia or the Middle East, then immediately connect onto another flight bound for Europe. The journey typically takes around 24 hours – or longer if a one-stop itinerary isn’t available.

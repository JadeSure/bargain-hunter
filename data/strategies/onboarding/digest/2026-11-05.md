### ING increases Savings Booster to 5.90% p.a. following RBA rate hike
- source: reddit · relevance 2 · <https://www.reddit.com/r/AusFinance/comments/1xnjm6y/ing_increases_savings_booster_to_590_pa_following/> · 2026-11-04

Following the RBA's 0.25% hike to 4.85% on 3 November, ING is raising the Savings Booster rate by 0.25% p.a. from 13 November. New savings rates: 5.90% p.a. on balances up to $500,000, up from 5.65%. That's the 2.75% base rate plus the 3.15% Boost rate. 6.50% p.a. for the first 4 months for new customers, up from 6.25%. The 0.60% Welcome rate is added on top. 2.75% p.a. on the part of a balance from $500,000 to $2 million (base rate only). Anything above $2 million earns 0%. Conditions: The Boost rate applies in any calendar month where the balance is at least $100 higher at the end of the month than at the end of the previous month. Interest earned doesn't count towards the $100. The Welcome rate is only for people who haven't held an ING personal savings account before, including any joint holder. You need an active Orange Everyday in the same name to open a Savings Booster. In a month where the balance doesn't grow by $100, you get only the 2.75% base rate.

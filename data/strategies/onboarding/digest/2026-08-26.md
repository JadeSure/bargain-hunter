### Has getting a partner always been financial advice on here?

So many people ask questions about how to buy a house alone etc, heck some are one 120K which isn’t super high but isn’t low. They’ll always be comments saying “get a partner”. We’re in a time we’re not everyone couples up anymore and certainly not by house buying age (25-29ish). So what are those people meant to do? Getting a partner is all good and well to increase borrowing power etc but we all know how that can go if you divorce/break up with sharers assets. Maybe this sub skews older or more conservative, but just realised the advice isn’t reflective of what’s happening in the real world. I want to be financially successful and I don’t want that to hinge on whether I go on hinge* and find a partner. *dating app EDIt: I’m not against getting into a relationship! Please stop saying that! I’m against starting one just for financial reasons.

### How do you handle sweat equity in an existing Australian company without a massive tax bill?
- source: reddit · relevance 1 · <https://www.reddit.com/r/AusFinance/comments/1woy0wb/how_do_you_handle_sweat_equity_in_an_existing/> · 2026-09-24

I've been involved in a number of startups, and forming a company with someone from day one is relatively straightforward. However, I've had a few situations where I've been asked to join an existing company in exchange for sweat equity. The problem is the potential tax implications. If I'm issued shares in exchange for my work, I could end up with a significant tax bill despite not receiving any actual income. Worse still, those shares might not be sellable for years and could ultimately end up being worth nothing. I'm curious how others have structured these arrangements. Surely this is a fairly common situation when bringing a technical co-founder or new partner into an existing business? Has anyone been through something similar, or can recommend someone who specialises in this area? My accountant has suggested I speak to a specialist who deals with startup equity and these sorts of arrangements. Ideally, I'm looking for someone in Australia who has practical experience structuring these deals, rather than just general tax advice.

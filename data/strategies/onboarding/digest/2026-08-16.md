### The GST debate and the Gas Tax debate are the same thing.
- source: reddit · relevance 1 · <https://www.reddit.com/r/AusFinance/comments/1voryns/the_gst_debate_and_the_gas_tax_debate_are_the/> · 2026-08-15

Distinguishing between these creates a false narrative. Yes, WA does well, but they need to be able to invest in mining related infrastructure and get rewarded. Yes, other states should be able to get an equivalent standard of living and not be given artificial incentives to migrate. So, the simple solution is to more or less keep the current arrangements and the federal govt to get a proper (Norway style?) return on or resources, particularly natural gas. There is more than enough money for all the things - we are an absurdly rich country. I can't see this being talked about as a co-joined issue. submitted by /u/Vryl [link] [comments]
